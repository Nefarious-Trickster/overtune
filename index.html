Powers and add-ons

Every hero has a signature power on right click. Before a match, slot two add-ons into it. Each add-on bends the power: more snares, longer blinks, a ram that ploughs through a whole team, a fade that lets you shoot while invisible.

Add-ons come in five rarities. Common and Uncommon ones are clean upgrades. Rare and Very Rare ones add new rules. Iridescent ones transform the power — and always cost you something for it.

Modes

Team Deathmatch — first team to the kill target. Control — capture the zone and hold it to 100%. Elimination — no respawns, first to three rounds. Free-for-All — first to 15.

Jump pads (blue rings) throw you onto high ground. Green crosses restore 75 health.
